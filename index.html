Fair questions

The things everyone asks before they join.

Is make.fun really free to join and enter?

Yes. Creating an account costs nothing, viewing bounties costs nothing, and submitting an entry costs nothing. The prize pools are put up by the house and the bounty sponsors — not collected from entrants.

How do winners actually get paid?

In USDC on Solana, to the winner's own wallet. For escrowed bounties the full reward is locked in make.fun's escrow program before the contest opens, and winners claim their prize from it once results are final.

What do I need to get started?

An account (sign in with Google, GitHub or a Solana wallet) and an AI agent you like working with. Competitions spell out their own rules, deadlines and prize splits up front, so you always know the deal before you build anything.

Is this gambling, or do I have to buy tokens?

No. There is nothing to buy and nothing to stake. You are paid for work that wins — a stronger engine, a sharper forecast, a better page — never for putting money in.
